Pandafy Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Who we are

Pandafy is an Android PDF utility provided by Aayush Yash. This policy explains how the Android app, identified as com.ay.pandafy, handles your information. For privacy questions, contact aayash2002@gmail.com.

Documents and information you use in Pandafy

Pandafy accesses PDF documents and images to provide the functions you use, such as reading, searching within PDFs, organizing pages, merging, splitting, compressing, converting, scanning, annotating, signing, protecting and unlocking documents. Documents and images may contain personal or sensitive information. Pandafy also uses document names, file locations or provider references, sizes, modification dates and page counts where available.

Pandafy keeps local library information, including favorites, opening history, bookmarks, saved output records and approved save locations. Its filesystem browser creates a local index of PDF names, folder paths, sizes and modification dates across accessible internal shared storage. This index supports browsing, Recent and filename search; indexing does not search document contents. Visible document thumbnails and PDF text search use local document processing.

Preferences, an optional display name, onboarding status, and permission-related state are stored on your device. The display name is for local personalization; it does not create an account.

Permissions and file access

All files access allows Pandafy to discover PDFs across accessible folders in your device's primary internal shared storage, browse folders, search filenames and open documents for local tools. Android-protected folders and other apps' private data remain restricted. You can grant or revoke this access in Android Settings. Without it, the built-in filesystem browser is unavailable; Android document-provider references use their own grants.

Camera access is used when you choose to scan documents. It is not needed to use existing PDFs. You can deny or revoke it in Android Settings. Gallery images are selected through Android's image or document picker. Pandafy does not request microphone access or broad photo-library permission in the reviewed release configuration.

Android's document picker and approved folder grants allow Pandafy to read selected files and save outputs. Access may expire or be revoked, requiring you to select a file or location again. A third-party dependency also declares permission to observe network connection state; this permission does not itself grant Internet access.

On-device processing, passwords and signatures

PDF and image operations run on your device using bundled PDF libraries and Android image APIs. The reviewed release configuration does not include an Internet permission, and the app has no configured document-upload server, analytics, advertising, account service or crash-report upload service.

PDF passwords are used locally to open, protect or unlock documents. The app does not intentionally save passwords to its preferences or recovery records. Passwords and decoded document data exist in memory during use; complete erasure of every memory copy cannot be guaranteed. Protect PDF creates a password-encrypted PDF. Unlock PDF creates a document without its former password protection. Other editing or page-composition tools can also create unprotected copies of encrypted inputs after confirmation. Temporary or generated unprotected documents are readable without that password.

If you save a visual signature, Pandafy stores its name and image in private app storage so you can reuse it. You can remove saved signatures in Settings. Signatures and annotations become part of documents when you save them. A visual signature is an appearance, not a certificate-based digital signature.

Temporary files, drafts and outputs

Pandafy uses private working storage for PDF staging, previews, scan originals and edits, generated outputs, save/retry records and replacement recovery. Scan drafts can survive app restarts so you can resume work. Failed or interrupted operations may retain working files until you complete, discard or resolve the operation. These files can contain document contents, images, filenames and file references. Working storage is not automatically password-encrypted merely because it is private.

Most tools save a new output to the location you choose. Some existing private outputs may also remain in app storage. Protect/Unlock can replace an eligible local original only after your explicit confirmation. Replacement uses temporary files and recovery records, and may retain a copy of the original if recovery is uncertain. That backup preserves the original's existing encryption state; it is not necessarily encrypted. Other normal copy operations preserve source PDFs and source photos. Camera capture cache files may be removed after admission into the durable draft.

An external save location may be local storage or a cloud-backed Android document provider. Cloud-backed locations are services outside Pandafy, even when Android presents them in the system picker.

Transfers to other apps, providers and backup services

When you choose Share or an external document provider, Pandafy gives the selected app or provider access to the files needed for that action. It may receive document contents, names and related metadata. Receiving apps and cloud providers may upload, retain or further share that information under their own policies. Pandafy does not control their retention or transport security.

Android backup and device-transfer services may copy eligible private app data according to your device settings and service policies. The reviewed app does not explicitly disable system backup. Eligible data may include saved signatures, preferences, library metadata and legacy private documents. Cache and designated no-backup working directories are excluded from standard Android Auto Backup. Device and service behavior can vary.

The app uses third-party libraries for its interface, local database, camera, PDF processing and Android integration. These are bundled software components rather than an account or cloud-processing requirement. No analytics, advertising, authentication or billing integration is configured in the reviewed app. Android, Google Play and services you choose operate under their own privacy policies.

The privacy-policy webpage itself has no analytics, tracking scripts, remote fonts or third-party assets. Its hosting provider may process normal website requests, including IP addresses and browser request information, under its own policy.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences, saved signatures, bookmarks, library records, output history and unfinished work can persist across restarts. There is no fixed time limit for all of this information.

Removing a document from Pandafy's library removes its library reference, not the source file. Separate processed-output records, bookmarks, provider grants or recoverable working data can remain. The filesystem browser may discover the same existing PDF again. Removing a favorite only changes the favorite status. Removing an approved save location does not delete the files there.

Clear Temporary Storage currently removes eligible sharing-copy folders older than 72 hours when cleanup runs. It does not erase all caches, scan drafts, unfinished outputs, signatures, history or replacement recovery files. Android may independently reclaim cache files. Discard drafts or unfinished jobs through their available controls, and resolve replacement recovery before clearing app data.

Clearing app storage in Android Settings or uninstalling normally removes private app data, including private drafts and recovery material. Files saved in shared storage, files held by other apps, cloud outputs and system backups are not deleted by removing a library reference or uninstalling Pandafy. Delete those through the relevant file manager, recipient, provider or backup service. System restore may bring back eligible backed-up app data.

Security

Pandafy uses Android's private app storage for internal records and working files, limits temporary sharing access through Android file grants, and validates generated PDF outputs before completing its save workflows. General app metadata, saved signatures and working files do not have an additional app-level encryption layer. Document encryption is applied when you choose Protect PDF. Device security, storage providers, backups and receiving apps also affect confidentiality. No storage or processing system can guarantee complete security or forensic erasure.

Children and audience

Pandafy provides document utilities without an app account or age-profile system. Documents used by children can contain sensitive information just like other documents; the same handling described here applies. A parent or guardian with privacy questions can use the contact below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or its data handling changes. The current version and effective date will be available on this page. Material changes will be communicated as appropriate to the change and applicable requirements.
